**Checked on 2026-10-26. Always re-check against the latest FTA / Ministry of Finance publications before use. If any newer official source differs, the newer source wins.**

| Phase | Who | ASP appointment by | Go-live | Status of this row |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pilot | volunteers | — | 1 July 2026 | per Ecovis (secondary source) |

| Government | B2G / government entities | 31 March 2027 | **1 October 2027** | per Ecovis; ASP date **to verify** |

## Model
- Peppol network, decentralised continuous transaction control and exchange ("5-corner" model).
- Structured XML/JSON using UBL / **PINT AE**. PDFs and scanned documents no longer qualify.
- Each business appoints an **FTA-accredited service provider (ASP)** connected to Peppol; the ASP validates invoices, exchanges them with the buyer's ASP and reports tax data to the FTA in near real time.
